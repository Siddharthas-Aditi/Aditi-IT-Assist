# Problem Statement — Aditi IT Assist

## Context

Aditi Consulting supports thousands of employees across multiple geographies. The internal
IT support team handles a significant volume of tickets daily, many of which involve
repetitive, well-documented issues that follow predictable resolution patterns.

## Core Problems

### 1. Repetitive Issue Overload
Over 60% of IT support tickets fall into categories with known solutions:
- Email/Outlook configuration issues
- Zoom sign-in and audio problems
- Intune device compliance
- Hardware camera access
- VPN connectivity

These consume disproportionate support staff time.

### 2. Knowledge Accessibility Gap
Solutions exist in documentation, wikis, and tribal knowledge — but employees can't
find them at the moment of need. The gap between "solution exists" and "employee can
apply it" creates unnecessary ticket volume.

### 3. Inconsistent Resolution Quality
Different support agents provide different quality of guidance. There's no standardized
workflow ensuring best practices are followed for each issue category.

### 4. Slow Resolution Times
Average ticket resolution for common issues takes 4-24 hours due to queue times,
agent availability, and back-and-forth communication.

### 5. No Systematic Learning
When new solutions are discovered or existing solutions improve, there's no efficient
mechanism to feed this back into the support process.

## Impact

- Employee productivity loss during IT issues
- IT staff burnout from repetitive work
- Delayed onboarding when new hires face common issues
- Compounding costs as organization grows

## Constraints

- Must work within existing security and compliance framework
- Must not expose sensitive data to external systems without approval
- Must support graceful degradation when AI is uncertain
- Must maintain human oversight for complex issues
- Must integrate with enterprise identity (future phase)
